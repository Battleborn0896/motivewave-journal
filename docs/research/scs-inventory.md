# SCS Trading Journal (SCS / "Sierra Chart Studies", scstudies.com) - Windows desktop journal app fed by a companion Sierra Chart ACSIL study (and a NinjaTrader 8 AddOn): feature inventory

Compiled from public pages on 2026-10-10 as a checklist for independent implementation. Behaviour is summarised in our own words; no vendor code, text or assets are used.

## Overview

SCS (scstudies.com; the NexusFi directory calls it "SCS Studies") is a one-developer shop founded in 2024 (the developer is called Charles in reviews and in the NexusFi listing). Its Trading Journal is a standalone Windows 10+ desktop application (Electron + TypeScript, local SQLite database, no cloud, no telemetry) that is fed automatically by a companion Sierra Chart custom study named TRADING_JOURNAL. Since mid-2026 the same app also takes trades from NinjaTrader 8 through a companion AddOn. The journal is its own app with seven tabs (Dashboard, Trades, Gallery, Edge, Calendar, Diary, Settings); the chart-side study only exports events, takes screenshots, draws a rules panel and exposes a couple of buttons.

What sets it apart, per the vendor: one MP4 video clip per trade from a rolling screen-recording buffer; automatic chart screenshots on every fill (typically 4-6 per trade, optionally one PNG per chart in the chartbook); tick-level MFE/MAE and intra-trade PnL curves rebuilt from Sierra Chart .scid files; post-exit MFE tracking (Captured %, Missed Profit); rules that are shown live on the chart and can hard-block new live entries through the separate Trade Manager study; prop-firm buffer/target tiles; "Edges" defined as named saved-filter combinations; a Diary with Cost of Mistakes; missed-trade logging; Monte Carlo; CSV/PDF export; backup/restore.

Almost everything below comes from the vendor's own product page and blog (first-party marketing, very detailed). No public documentation site, changelog page or YouTube videos were found (/docs and /changelog return 404; the product page refers to "the documentation" and an in-app changelog without linking them). The NexusFi listing returned HTTP 403 to direct fetches, so its content is known only through search-engine snippets; those snippets are consistent with the vendor site (auto-screenshot on each fill, per-trade video, post-exit MFE, prop-firm drawdown buffer and profit target, Edge tab, native Sierra Chart streaming via NDJSON, bundle with Trade Manager, 14 catalog studies and 2 desktop apps, 5.0 rating). No independent reviews (Reddit, Elite Trader, Sierra Chart support board) were found; the only user voices are testimonials hosted on the vendor's site.

Pricing as far as visible: subscription, monthly or yearly, plus a "Trading Journal: Lifetime" licence that exists as a SKU. Bundle with Trade Manager is $19.99/mo versus $23.68/mo separately; Trade Manager alone is $6.69/mo, which implies about $16.99/mo for the journal alone (derived, not printed). A March 2026 vendor blog post quoted EUR 14.49/mo. Homepage says every product has a 7-day free trial (no card); lifetime purchases have a 7-day refund window.

## How trades reach the journal

TRANSPORT (Sierra Chart): file based, one direction is documented. A custom ACSIL study called TRADING_JOURNAL is added to a chart in Sierra Chart. As trading events happen it appends NDJSON (newline-delimited JSON) lines to files inside a folder the vendor calls the "Journal Directory". The desktop app has a folder watcher that polls that directory and imports new rows every 5 seconds. The vendor's marketing calls this "native streaming", but the mechanism described is files plus polling; no socket, port, DTC, HTTP or shared-memory channel is mentioned anywhere for the journal (the separate Trade Copier product does use Windows shared memory, the journal does not claim to). Every NDJSON line carries UTC epoch timestamps plus the chart's timezone offset, so a chart set to Chicago time on a PC in another zone still lines up with clips and screenshots.

WHAT THE USER SETS UP: (1) buy/activate; Sierra Chart studies from this vendor are authorised against the user's Sierra Chart account name and simply appear under Custom Studies after a restart (no DLL download) - stated on the support page for studies generally; the journal purchase "includes the Sierra Chart study + NinjaTrader connector". (2) Install the signed Windows app and activate it online with a licence key. (3) Add the TRADING_JOURNAL study to a chart (accounts are streamed by "their own chart-hosted study"). (4) Point the app at the Sierra Chart journal folder (Settings > Data; extra folders can be added as "Additional Journal Directories" so several Sierra Chart instances are watched in parallel). (5) Optionally point the app at the Sierra Chart Data folder so it can find each symbol's .scid tick file. (6) Optionally pick a Media Storage Folder for screenshots and clips, and switch on Video Capture. A vendor blog admits setup takes more than five minutes.

CAPTURED AUTOMATICALLY BY THE STUDY: fills and trades (grouped flat-to-flat, quantity-weighted entry/exit), the originating trade account, open positions (they show up in the app as OPEN rows before the exit and reconcile on the final fill), stops (including the stop line drawn with the vendor's Trade Manager study, which becomes the trade's initial-risk reference), tick-by-tick MFE and MAE from a tracker inside the study, optional post-exit tracking (post-exit peak, a trigger reason, optional screenshot; forward-only, must be on before the trade closes; end-of-day time uses the Sierra Chart PC's local clock), and events that drive video capture (a study input "Enable Video Capture Events" turns these off per study instance). Whether targets are exported is not stated.

SCREENSHOTS: taken on the Sierra Chart side by the study, automatically (Off / All Fills / First and Last Fill) or on demand from an ACS control-bar button. Capture scope: Chart Window, Chartbook (one PNG per chart), Chartbook Symbol Matching, Main Sierra Chart Window, or Desktop. Triangle fill markers (small/medium/large) are drawn on the chart. Optional end-of-day screenshot per trade at a set time. The app's diagnostic checks "the journal directory, auto folder, matching files, DB references", which implies PNGs land in a subfolder of the journal directory and are matched to trades by the app; the exact naming/matching key is not published.

VIDEO: recorded by the desktop app, not by the study. The app runs a bundled ffmpeg screen recorder in the background with a rolling buffer (default 30 s), and when the NDJSON stream shows a trade opening/closing it cuts one MP4 per trade covering the pre-entry buffer, the whole trade and a configurable post-close window. Modes: Off (default), Manual (a "Video Clip" button on the study toggles a clip; on stop the user picks the trade to attach it to), Auto on trade. Windows only.

TICK DATA: independently of the NDJSON feed, the app reads Sierra Chart's binary .scid files to rebuild each trade's tick-by-tick PnL curve with FIFO lot tracking and to recompute MFE/MAE; study values, SCID recomputed values and manual overrides are stored separately and the user can promote the SCID value.

REVERSE DIRECTION (app to chart): the app evaluates the user's rules continuously; the study draws a "Rules & Risk Limits" panel on the chart (one line per rule with current value vs limit, title goes red on breach, optional sound, rule named in the Sierra Chart Alerts Log), and the app "signals" the separate Trade Manager study so that rules marked hard-stop block new entries on live accounts. A "View on Chart"/replay button in the app makes Sierra Chart jump to the trade's entry date-time with fill markers, and a "Back to Live" pill in the app releases the frozen chart. The channel used for all of this (file, flag, other) is not disclosed.

FALLBACK / BACKFILL PATH: Data > Import SC TradesList... takes the tab-separated .txt exported from Sierra Chart's Trade Activity Log, in Trades mode (flat-to-flat rows) or Fills mode (raw fills the app regroups). There is a re-import-last button and an option to auto re-import that file whenever Sierra Chart overwrites it (duplicate-safe; recommended for order-allocation sibling accounts that have no study of their own). A "Sync Sierra Chart trades" item also sits in the Data menu (undocumented). Deleted trades are remembered in a tombstone table so the watcher never resurrects them from the NDJSON files.

LIVE vs SIM vs REPLAY: accounts carry a "Simulated" flag that the user sets in the app; trades on simulated accounts are journaled but excluded from live Rules & Risk counters and from hard-stop blocking. A vendor blog says trades taken in Sierra Chart Replay are captured the same way as live trades but are NOT automatically tagged as replay; the advice is to use a dedicated account name or a note and filter on it. How replay timestamps are stored is not stated.

NINJATRADER: a companion AddOn (downloaded from the user's account, copied into NT8, auto-detected and auto-updated by the app) streams executions; transport not stated. SCID-based tick curves are Sierra Chart only.

DESKTOP APP SCREENS: top bar with filters (date range, symbol, direction, multi-select account, strategy, trade checklist, session checklist, Trade Type real/missed), an R/$ switch, a "+" add-trade button (with Missed option), a Data menu (Import SC TradesList..., re-import, Sync Sierra Chart trades, Export Trades to CSV...) and the Back to Live pill. Tabs: Dashboard (20 KPI cards, 14 charts, sticky notes, mini-calendar, heatmaps, checklist correlation, Edge Performance and PropFirm panes); Trades (table, Advanced Filter Builder, bulk actions, Trade Detail panel, Edit Trade form, screenshot lightbox, trade comparison); Gallery (screenshot card grid); Edge (per-edge cards and detail); Calendar (yearly heatmap, monthly calendar, Session Detail panel); Diary (session checklist days, Cost of Mistakes); Settings (split page: Rules & Risk Limits on the left; on the right collapsible sections Show/Hide, Rules, Edge Items, PropFirm, Accounts, Strategies, Checklists, Commissions, Trading, Data, Display, Screenshot, Video Capture, License, Update). Plus a "What's New" popup after updates.

## Features

### capture

- **Companion Sierra Chart study writing NDJSON** (high): A custom ACSIL study named TRADING_JOURNAL sits on a chart and writes trade events as NDJSON lines into a journal folder as they occur: trades, fills, stops, study-tracked MFE/MAE, open-trade state. No manual export step. The vendor markets this as native streaming, but the mechanism described is files in a folder. [source](https://www.scstudies.com/trading-journal)
- **Folder watcher with 5-second import** (high): The desktop app watches the Journal Directory and imports any new rows every 5 seconds. Import is automatic once the folder is configured; nothing to click. [source](https://www.scstudies.com/trading-journal)
- **Additional Journal Directories (multiple Sierra Chart instances)** (high): Under Settings > Data the user can add extra journal folders; all are watched in parallel so several Sierra Chart installations/instances feed one journal. [source](https://www.scstudies.com/trading-journal)
- **Live open trades** (high): A position appears in the Trades table as an OPEN row as soon as it is entered, so the user can type notes and tick checklist items while still in the trade. The row reconciles into a closed trade when the final fill arrives. [source](https://www.scstudies.com/trading-journal)
- **Flat-to-flat trade grouping** (high): A trade is everything between flat and flat. Scale-ins, partial exits and multiple stops/targets are merged into one trade with quantity-weighted entry and exit prices; the user never merges or splits executions by hand. PnL curves use FIFO lot matching. [source](https://www.scstudies.com/trading-journal)
- **Stop capture and Trade Manager stop handoff** (high): The stop the trade opened with is recorded and used as Initial Risk. When the entry is placed with the vendor's separate Trade Manager study, the horizontal stop line drawn on the chart is passed to the journal automatically, so R, MFE and MAE in R need no manual input. Without Trade Manager, R relies on whatever stop/risk data arrives with the import. [source](https://www.scstudies.com/products/trade-manager-trading-journal-bundle)
- **Study-side tick MFE/MAE tracker** (high): The Sierra Chart study tracks maximum favourable and adverse excursion tick by tick while the trade is open and exports the values with the trade. These are kept as the 'study' source alongside SCID-recomputed and manual values. [source](https://www.scstudies.com/trading-journal)
- **Post-exit MFE tracking** (high): Optional. When switched on before a trade closes, the study keeps watching price after the exit and reports a post-exit peak, a trigger reason for why tracking ended, and optionally a screenshot. Trade Detail then shows Post-Exit MFE, Captured % and Missed Profit. Forward-only: trades already closed cannot be backfilled. The end-of-day cut-off uses the Sierra Chart PC's local clock with no timezone conversion. The list of trigger reasons and the tracking window are not published. [source](https://www.scstudies.com/trading-journal)
- **UTC timestamps plus chart timezone offset** (high): Every NDJSON line includes UTC epoch time and the chart's timezone offset so trades, screenshots and video clips line up even when chart timezone and PC timezone differ. [source](https://www.scstudies.com/trading-journal)
- **NinjaTrader 8 connector** (high): A companion AddOn streams NT8 executions into the same app. The app auto-detects the NT8 install; the user downloads the connector from their account, drops it into NinjaTrader and restarts; the connector updates itself with each app release and prompts for an AddOn restart. Same analytics and rule enforcement; no SCID tick curves on NT. Transport not disclosed. [source](https://www.scstudies.com/blog/best-ninjatrader-trading-journal-2026)
- **Multi-account capture with auto-registration** (high): Each imported trade records its originating account on every import path. A new account is created automatically the first time one of its trades arrives and is added to the active account filter so its trades do not silently disappear from view. [source](https://www.scstudies.com/trading-journal)
- **Order-allocation sibling and copy-trader accounts** (high): Accounts that receive allocated/copied fills but have no chart-hosted study of their own are covered by auto-importing the last TradesList file; this path is described as duplicate-safe. Multi-account exports are split defensively at account boundaries even when the flat-to-flat marker is missing, so trades do not leak between accounts. [source](https://www.scstudies.com/trading-journal)
- **Replay / simulated trade capture** (medium): Trades placed during Sierra Chart Replay are journaled the same way as live trades and get the same SCID-based analytics. They are not auto-tagged as replay; the vendor advises a dedicated account name or note so they can be filtered. Per-account 'Simulated' flag is set by the user in the app. [source](https://www.scstudies.com/blog/how-to-backtest-sierra-chart-study)
- **Deleted-trade tombstone** (high): Deleting a trade (single or bulk) is permanent, and a tombstone record stops the watcher from re-importing it even though the row still exists in the source NDJSON file. [source](https://www.scstudies.com/trading-journal)
- **Symbol convention recognition and micro detection** (high): Recognises three Sierra Chart symbol styles: Teton (e.g. ESH26_FUT_CME), Rithmic dot (ESM6.CME) and Rithmic dash (ES-202506). Micro contracts are identified by tick value rather than name. [source](https://www.scstudies.com/trading-journal)
- **Study delivery without DLL download** (medium): Vendor studies are authorised against the buyer's Sierra Chart username (Help > About, 'Registered To'); after purchase and a Sierra Chart restart the study shows up in Custom Studies and works on any PC logged into that Sierra Chart account. Stated for studies in general; the journal purchase is said to include the Sierra Chart study. The desktop app itself needs a separate licence key. [source](https://www.scstudies.com/support)

### trade data

- **Trade Detail panel** (high): Shows entry and exit price, time and order type; Initial Risk with optional override; final R; MFE and MAE with their active source; an intra-trade PnL curve; notes; trade checklist; reorderable screenshots; videos; top-5 similar trades. Metric tiles: Net PnL, Commission, Points, Duration, Efficiency, Entry/Exit/Stop, Qty. Any section or tile can be hidden in Settings. Previous/next via side arrows or left/right keys, following the table's current order. [source](https://www.scstudies.com/trading-journal)
- **R-value from per-trade Initial Risk** (high): R = realised result divided by the risk implied by the stop the trade opened with, so trades with different dollar risk stay comparable and changing risk later never rewrites history. An Initial Risk Override in dollars or percent of account can be set in the Edit Trade form for A/B setups or trades that arrived with no stop. [source](https://www.scstudies.com/trading-journal)
- **Default Risk mode and stop auto-fill** (high): Trading settings include a Default Risk mode of fixed ticks. With it, an optional stop auto-fill gives newly imported stop-less trades a visible stop at entry plus/minus the risk ticks, mainly so the stop can be seen and replayed. The default mode itself is not stated. [source](https://www.scstudies.com/trading-journal)
- **Break-even threshold, net R, recompute** (high): A break-even threshold (in $, % or ticks) decides what counts as a scratch trade. R can optionally be computed net of commissions. A 'Recompute R-values' action reapplies current risk settings to all existing trades. [source](https://www.scstudies.com/trading-journal)
- **MFE/MAE provenance and promote** (high): Three values are stored side by side per trade: the study tracker's, a recompute from SCID ticks, and an optional manual override. Trade Detail shows which is active when they disagree, and the user can promote the SCID value without losing the original. A display-format option for MFE/MAE lives in Settings > Display (formats not listed; a blog says they can be shown in R). [source](https://www.scstudies.com/trading-journal)
- **Tick-level intra-trade PnL curve** (high): The app reads Sierra Chart's binary .scid file for the trade's symbol (found automatically once the Data folder is set) and redraws the trade's PnL tick by tick with FIFO lot tracking for scale-ins and partials. Viewable in dollars, R or ticks. The curve is gross; KPI tiles are net. Sierra Chart only. [source](https://www.scstudies.com/trading-journal)
- **Efficiency, Captured % and Missed Profit** (medium): Trade Detail carries an Efficiency tile (how much of the available move was kept) and, when post-exit tracking ran, Captured % and Missed Profit. Formulas are not published. [source](https://www.scstudies.com/trading-journal)
- **Per-account, per-symbol commissions** (high): A searchable table holds dollars per round turn for each account/symbol pair. A toggle chooses whether to use the commissions reported by Sierra Chart instead. A Recompute button re-prices every existing trade. [source](https://www.scstudies.com/trading-journal)
- **Instrument table (50+ futures) and custom symbols** (high): Pre-configured tick size/value for index (ES/MES 0.25 = $12.50/$1.25, NQ/MNQ 0.25 = $5/$0.50, YM/MYM 1.00 = $5/$0.50, RTY/M2K 0.10 = $5/$0.50), CL/MCL (0.01 = $10/$1), GC/MGC (0.10 = $10/$1), bonds (ZB, UB, TN, ZN, ZF, ZT), currencies (6E, M6E, 6J, 6B, M6B, 6A, M6A, 6C, 6S, 6N, 6M), grains, meats, softs and crypto (BTC, MBT, ETH, MET). Users can add a custom symbol with its own tick size and tick value. Futures only. [source](https://www.scstudies.com/trading-journal)
- **Manual trades and Edit Trade** (medium): A '+' button adds a manual real trade; its menu also offers Missed. An Edit Trade form exists (includes the Initial Risk override). The full list of editable/manual fields is not published. [source](https://www.scstudies.com/trading-journal)
- **Missed trades** (high): Via '+' > Missed the user logs a setup seen but not taken: symbol, direction, entry time, theoretical stop, theoretical target, and the reason for skipping. The journal computes the hypothetical R and keeps a running opportunity-cost total. Missed trades live in the Trades workflow, are shown/hidden by the Trade Type filter, and can be included in Edges. [source](https://www.scstudies.com/trading-journal)
- **Account balance and cash ledger** (high): Each account has an initial balance and a date-only ledger of deposits, withdrawals and payouts that is independent of the toolbar date filter. The Account Balance KPI tooltip breaks down initial balance, net PnL, deposits, withdrawals and current value; the dashboard also shows growth %. [source](https://www.scstudies.com/trading-journal)
- **Per-account Simulated flag** (high): An account can be marked Simulated. Its trades are still journaled but never count toward live Rules & Risk counters and are never blocked by hard stops. [source](https://www.scstudies.com/trading-journal)

### screenshots and video

- **Auto-screenshot trigger** (high): Setting with three values: Off, All Fills, or First and Last Fill. With all fills, every entry and exit execution produces a capture, typically 4-6 images per trade. [source](https://www.scstudies.com/trading-journal)
- **Screenshot capture modes** (high): Five scopes: Chart Window (the chart hosting the study/focused chart), Chartbook (every chart in the chartbook, one PNG each), Chartbook Symbol Matching (only charts of the traded symbol), Main Sierra Chart Window, or the whole Desktop. [source](https://www.scstudies.com/trading-journal)
- **Manual screenshot button on the chart** (high): An ACS control-bar button on the study captures on demand. A setting decides whether a manual capture attaches to the latest trade on that chart's symbol or asks each time. [source](https://www.scstudies.com/trading-journal)
- **Fill markers on charts** (high): The study draws triangle markers at fills; size is small, medium or large. Fill markers also appear when a trade is reopened on the chart via replay. [source](https://www.scstudies.com/trading-journal)
- **End-of-day and post-exit screenshots** (high): Optional extra capture per trade at a set end-of-day time, showing how the day finished relative to the trade; post-exit tracking can also attach its own screenshot. [source](https://www.scstudies.com/trading-journal)
- **Manual image attachment (drag, browse, paste)** (high): Images can be dragged in from Explorer, picked with a multi-file browser, or pasted with Ctrl+V / by clicking a paste zone. Pasted images get the same file naming as picked ones. Works for trades and for sessions. [source](https://www.scstudies.com/trading-journal)
- **Screenshot reorder and lightbox** (high): Screenshots inside a trade can be dragged into a new order, which persists and decides the fallback gallery thumbnail. Clicking the camera badge or the Gallery magnifier opens a full-size lightbox navigated with left/right and closed with Esc. [source](https://www.scstudies.com/trading-journal)
- **Screenshot annotation tools** (low): One customer review says auto-screenshots come with built-in annotation tools. Not described anywhere in the vendor's own feature text. [source](https://www.scstudies.com/reviews)
- **Missing-screenshot diagnostic** (high): A Settings action checks the journal directory, the auto-capture folder, matching files, database references and per-trade files to explain why a trade has no screenshot. [source](https://www.scstudies.com/trading-journal)
- **Per-trade video capture (rolling buffer)** (high): Optional, Windows only, off by default. The app records the screen continuously into a rolling buffer (default 30 s) and, when a trade closes and a configurable post-close window has passed, assembles one MP4 covering pre-entry, the full trade (the buffer is kept per active trade, so long trades are whole) and the aftermath. Modes: Off, Manual, Auto on trade. [source](https://www.scstudies.com/trading-journal)
- **Manual video clip from the chart** (high): In Manual mode a 'Video Clip' button on the Sierra Chart study starts and stops a clip; on stop the user chooses which trade it belongs to. A study input 'Enable Video Capture Events' disables capture for that study instance. [source](https://www.scstudies.com/trading-journal)
- **Video encoding, scope and audio** (high): Bundled ffmpeg; detects NVIDIA NVENC, Intel QuickSync or AMD AMF and falls back to CPU. Capture scope: the display Sierra Chart is on (default), a fixed display, or all displays. Microphone narration optional; a silent audio track is written when no mic is available. [source](https://www.scstudies.com/trading-journal)
- **Video retention and diagnostics** (high): Clips are deleted after a retention period (default 90 days) and the rolling buffer has a disk cap (value not published). A diagnostics action reports ffmpeg status, devices, scope, encoder fallback, buffer state and recent errors. [source](https://www.scstudies.com/trading-journal)
- **Video playback in Trade Detail** (high): Clips play inside the trade with 0.5x-2x speed and a Jump-to-entry button. Hovering shows a trash icon; the user chooses between detaching the clip from the trade or also deleting the file. [source](https://www.scstudies.com/trading-journal)
- **Gallery thumbnail with custom crop** (high): For each trade the user can pick which screenshot is the card image and drag/resize a crop rectangle locked to the card's aspect ratio; changes save automatically. [source](https://www.scstudies.com/trading-journal)

### tagging

- **Strategy tags** (high): Simple named strategies created, renamed, deleted and reordered in Settings; one strategy per trade shown as a table column and toolbar filter; assignable in bulk. [source](https://www.scstudies.com/trading-journal)
- **Trade checklist items and gauges** (high): User-defined per-trade checklist items. An item is either a checkbox or a numeric gauge scored 1 to N, optionally lower-is-better; gauges appear as scored tags in Trade Detail, Session Detail and Diary. The table shows checklist completion per trade. [source](https://www.scstudies.com/trading-journal)
- **Session checklist sets** (high): Separate from trade checklists: items for session-level habits (pre-market prep, end-of-day review, emotional state), grouped into named, reorderable sets and browsed Set > Category > Item. Each session keeps its notes and screenshots separately per set. [source](https://www.scstudies.com/trading-journal)
- **Pre-trade checklist armed per symbol** (high): A separate entry-criteria checklist that the user ticks before entering. It is armed per symbol (several symbols at once) and the ticks attach to the next live trade on that symbol. The panel can be hidden in Show/Hide. Whether it is shown on the chart or only in the app is not stated. [source](https://www.scstudies.com/trading-journal)
- **Edges as saved-filter combinations** (high): An Edge is a named recipe combining checklist items, session-checklist items, symbols, directions, accounts and real/missed type, managed under Settings > Edge Items separately from Strategy items. Each has a description and cover image. [source](https://www.scstudies.com/trading-journal)
- **Notes at four levels plus sticky notes** (high): Free-text notes on trades, sessions (every calendar day has a pencil, even empty ones), weeks (WK column) and months (month header). Dashboard sticky notes hold pinned reminders. All note fields have multi-language spell check (English, French, German, Spanish and more) with right-click suggestions and add-to-dictionary. Notes being typed on an open trade are saved automatically when the trade closes. [source](https://www.scstudies.com/trading-journal)
- **Note keyword search and automatic tags** (low): A customer review mentions searching notes by keyword and 'automatic tags'. Neither is described in the vendor's feature text. [source](https://www.scstudies.com/reviews)
- **Bulk actions** (high): Select several trades and assign an account, a strategy or notes, archive them to an 'Old trades' bucket, or delete them permanently. [source](https://www.scstudies.com/trading-journal)

## Not confirmed

- NDJSON schema: field names, event types, file naming/rotation, and the default Journal Directory path are not published anywhere public.
- How state travels from the app back to Sierra Chart (rule values for the on-chart panel, hard-stop signal to Trade Manager, the View on Chart jump, the Video Clip button's start/stop) - the vendor only says it 'signals' in real time; no file, socket or port is named.
- Who writes screenshot PNGs and where: the study is said to capture them and a diagnostic mentions an 'auto folder' in the journal directory, but file naming and the key used to match images to trades are not stated.
- Whether one TRADING_JOURNAL study instance is needed per chart, per symbol, per account or per Sierra Chart instance; and the study's full input list (only 'Enable Video Capture Events', the rules-panel display inputs, marker size and the screenshot/video buttons are named).
- Whether trades taken while the desktop app is closed are caught up on next launch (implied by a file-based design, never stated).
- Whether profit targets are exported, and whether partial-fill detail beyond flat-to-flat grouping is shown in the UI.
- Post-exit tracking: the list of trigger reasons, how long tracking runs, defaults, and the formulas for Captured %, Missed Profit and Efficiency.
- PropFirm: what 'milestone reset' does, whether end-of-day trailing versus intraday trailing drawdown is distinguished, and whether per-firm presets exist. Only 'trailing or static' plus an optional trailing-to-static lock is stated.
- How a 'mistake' is tagged and how Cost of Mistakes is calculated.
- Defaults for many settings: Default Risk mode, break-even threshold, net-R on/off, video post-close window, buffer disk cap, auto-screenshot default, end-of-day time.
- What 'Sync Sierra Chart trades' in the Data menu does, and what 'micro-on-full chart replay' and the 'View on Chart replay style' options mean.
- Replay handling details: the vendor blog says replay trades are captured like live ones and are not auto-tagged, but how replay timestamps are stored and whether the Simulated flag is ever set automatically is not stated.
- NinjaTrader AddOn transport (the vendor says it 'streams' without naming a mechanism).
- Exact prices: the journal's own monthly, yearly and lifetime prices are not printed on the product page as fetched. $16.99/mo is derived from the bundle arithmetic; EUR 14.49/mo comes from a March 2026 blog post; a search snippet showed the bundle at EUR 16.99/mo versus $19.99/mo on the site (likely currency or date differences). Number of device activations per licence is not stated. The product page does not mention a trial although the homepage advertises a 7-day trial on every product.
- Features that appear only in customer reviews on the vendor's site and not in the vendor's own description: screenshot annotation tools, keyword search across notes, 'automatic tags', and a PDF report of selected trades.
- Internal inconsistencies on the vendor's pages: 19 vs 20 KPIs between blog posts; Diary described both as multi-select set visibility and as one active set at a time; post-exit tracking described as covering 'every trade' and also as forward-only; catalogue size given as 10, 11 or 14 studies on different pages; 'no sync' on the product page versus a blog suggestion to sync the SQLite file via Dropbox.
- The NexusFi listing could not be read directly (HTTP 403 on nexusfi.com and futures.io); its content is known only from search-engine snippets, so anything attributed to it is third-party and unverified. It adds nothing beyond the vendor's own claims.
- No public documentation site, changelog page or video walkthroughs were found: /docs and /changelog return 404, the product page mentions 'the documentation' and an in-app changelog without links, and searches for YouTube, Reddit, Elite Trader and Sierra Chart support-board threads returned nothing about this product. The Discord server (discord.gg/8agxJx6wRN) was not read.
- All behaviour described is vendor marketing or vendor-hosted testimonials; nothing was verified against the running software, and no independent review was found.
